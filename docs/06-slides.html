Slides

A lecture deck built from the same kind of text file

In this chapter

  • Decide whether a course needs slides at all
  • Open, edit and preview the slide deck that comes with the template
  • Structure slides, add speaker notes and reveal content step by step
  • Create a second deck, and link to it
  • Export slides as a PDF

1 Do you need slides?

Not every course does. The chapters are meant to be read on their own. Slides are for a different situation: you presenting live, in a lecture or a webinar, where learners need something to look at while you talk.

If you would only be copying the chapter text onto slides, skip them.

2 The deck that comes with the template

Open slides/lecture-01.qmd. It is a text file like a chapter, and it turns into a full-screen presentation. It has the university’s logo top right and the blue rule under each slide title, so it matches the course.

To see it, render the website as usual and click Slides in the top menu. The deck opens in a new tab, and a click anywhere then the arrow keys move through it.

Useful keys while presenting

Arrow keys move between slides, F goes full-screen, S opens the speaker view with your notes and a timer, O shows an overview of all slides, and Esc leaves the overview.

3 Structure

The top of the file, between the two lines of dashes, has the title and settings. Leave the settings as they are and change only title, subtitle and author. Below it, every ## heading starts a new slide:

## The problem

Learners forget most of what they hear once.

## Our approach

- Explain briefly
- Let them try
- Ask a question

Compare with a chapter: the same headings and lists, only shorter. A slide should hold one idea and no more than about six lines.

Reveal step by step

To show content one piece at a time, put a line containing . . . (three dots separated by spaces) where the pause should be:

## Question

What is the first step?

. . .

**Render the website.**

Speaker notes

Notes are visible to you only, in the speaker view (S):

## Our approach

- Explain briefly
- Let them try

::: {.notes}
Mention the example from last week here. Two minutes.
:::

Two columns, images, emphasis

:::: {.columns}
::: {.column width="50%"}
Text on the left.
:::
::: {.column width="50%"}
![Caption](../images/placeholder.svg)
:::
::::

Notice the ../ before images. The slides live one folder deeper than the chapters, so they have to step up out of it to find the pictures.

Two coloured boxes are ready-made for emphasis: ::: {.callout-blue} (white text on blue) and ::: {.callout-yellow} (black text on yellow), both used as a normal block with a closing :::. Use them sparingly.

4 Slides that work

  • One idea per slide. If you need “and” in the heading, make two slides.
  • Few words, large. If it cannot be read from the back of the room, cut it.
  • Do not read the slide aloud. Put the detail in the chapter and the point on the slide.
  • Use pictures for what a picture shows better than words, with alt text as in Chapter 4.
  • End with what to do, not with “Thank you” or “Questions?”.

5 A second deck

  1. In the Files panel, open the slides folder. Copy lecture-01.qmd and name the copy lecture-02.qmd.
  2. Change its title and slides, and render the website.
  3. Link to it from where learners will look for it: from a chapter with [Lecture 2](slides/lecture-02.qmd), or (from within the slides folder itself) simply lecture-02.qmd. You can also add it to the top menu in _quarto.yml, under navbar:, in the same way as the existing Slides entry.

6 A PDF for handouts

Open the slides in the browser and add ?print-pdf to the end of the address, so it ends in lecture-01.html?print-pdf. Press Enter, then use the browser’s print function and choose Save as PDF. In the print dialog, set Margins to None and tick Background graphics. The PDF then keeps the colours and the logo.

7 Check your understanding

What starts a new slide?

Every second-level heading starts a new slide, exactly as it starts a new section in a chapter.

A slide has ten bullet points in small print. What is the best repair?

A slide supports the speaker; it is not a document. Cut it down and let the chapter carry the detail.

You want a picture on a slide. The file is images/diagram.png in the project. What is the link in the slide file?

The slide files live in the slides folder, one level below the project’s main folder, so they must step up with ../ to reach the images.

To show a slide’s content one piece at a time, you put a line with the characters ? at the point of the pause. (Type them with spaces.)
